Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. This policy describes how PCTweaker handles information when you visit the website.

Information you provide

PCTweaker does not require an account to read its guides or use its browser tools. If you contact us, we may receive the information you voluntarily provide, such as your name, email address, PC specifications or message content. We use that information to respond and improve the site.

Technical information

Hosting and security systems may process technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information and request timestamps for security, reliability and service delivery.

Analytics

PCTweaker uses Google Analytics measurement on pages where the analytics tag is installed. Analytics can process information about pages visited, approximate device characteristics and general usage events. Where consent is legally required, the applicable consent controls determine whether analytics technologies are used.

Advertising

PCTweaker has Google AdSense code installed for site connection and review. If the site is approved and advertising is served, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies for ad delivery, measurement and personalization subject to applicable law and the consent choices available to visitors.

European users and consent

Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland may be subject to Google's consent requirements when advertising is served. PCTweaker will use the applicable consent-management configuration for advertising before serving ads that require consent. Google provides its European regulations consent tools through AdSense Privacy & messaging.

External links

PCTweaker links to third-party sites, including official software, driver and documentation resources. Those sites have their own privacy policies and are responsible for their own processing.

Browser tools

Some PCTweaker tools process information locally in your browser, such as hardware details exposed by browser APIs or benchmark entries stored in local storage. The site does not require an account for these tools and the tools are designed not to upload those local measurements to PCTweaker servers.

Data retention and requests

Information sent directly to PCTweaker is retained only as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was provided, legal obligations or legitimate site administration. To ask about personal information you have sent to us, use the contact page.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the site's services, analytics or advertising configuration changes.
