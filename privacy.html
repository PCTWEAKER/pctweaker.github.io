Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026. This policy describes how PCTweaker handles information when you visit the website.

Information you provide

PCTweaker does not require an account to read its guides. If you contact us, we may receive the information you voluntarily provide, such as your name, email address, PC specifications or message content. We use that information to respond and improve the site.

Technical information

Like most websites, hosting and security systems may process technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information and request timestamps for security, reliability and service delivery.

Analytics

If analytics are enabled, we may process information about pages visited, approximate device characteristics and general usage events to understand which guides are useful and improve performance. Analytics will only be enabled according to the applicable consent requirements.

Advertising

If Google AdSense is enabled, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure and personalize advertisements, subject to applicable law and the choices provided through the site's consent mechanism. Advertising personalization and cookie choices may be managed through the controls made available by Google and the website.

European users and consent

For visitors in regions where consent is required for advertising or analytics technologies, PCTweaker will use the applicable consent mechanism before processing that requires consent. The available choices may include accepting, rejecting or managing purposes depending on the service configuration.

External links

PCTweaker links to third-party sites, including official software and driver resources. Those sites have their own privacy policies and are responsible for their own processing.

Data retention and requests

Information sent directly to PCTweaker is retained only as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was provided, legal obligations or legitimate site administration. To ask about personal information you have sent to us, use the contact page.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the site's services, analytics or advertising configuration changes.
