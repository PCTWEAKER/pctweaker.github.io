Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 2026. This page explains how cookies and similar technologies may be used on PCTweaker.

What cookies are

Cookies are small files or similar identifiers stored on a device by a website. They can be used for essential functionality, preferences, analytics or advertising.

Analytics

PCTweaker uses Google Analytics measurement on pages where the analytics tag is installed. Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies to understand traffic and site performance, subject to applicable consent requirements.

Advertising

PCTweaker has Google AdSense code installed for site connection and review. If advertising is approved and served, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies for advertising, measurement and personalization according to their policies and the choices available to visitors.

Your choices

Browser settings can usually block or delete cookies. Where consent is legally required for advertising or measurement, PCTweaker will provide the applicable consent controls before using technologies that require consent. Google also provides privacy and consent controls through its advertising services.

Local storage in PCTweaker tools

The Lab and benchmark tools may store profiles or benchmark entries in your browser's local storage so they remain available on that device. These entries are not required for normal site navigation and can be cleared through the relevant tool controls or your browser settings.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the site's technology, advertising or analytics configuration changes.
